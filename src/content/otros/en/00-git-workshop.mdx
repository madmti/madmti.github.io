During the _second semester of 2026_, I teach the **Git Fundamentals and Architecture** workshop at the university: a journey that starts with the history of version control systems and reaches Git's _internals_ — the object model, the DAG, packfiles, and advanced workflow tools.

<br />

The workshop is organized into _15 capsules_ (12 classes + 3 extras) with theory material and hands-on exercises. It starts on **August 13** and is taught **twice a week**, in person at the university's [_Labcomp_](https://www.labcomp.cl/), with simultaneous live streaming.

> Note: the workshop is currently available in Spanish only.

### Content
- **Fundamentals**: history of VCS, snapshots, working areas, and the basic flow (_init, add, commit, log, diff, restore_).
- **Internals**: the object model (_blob, tree, commit_), references, HEAD and the DAG, packfiles, and maintenance and recovery with _gc, reflog, and fsck_.
- **Advanced workflow**: branching and merging, remote branches, rebase, revision selection and ranges, interactive staging, stash, history rewriting, and demystifying _reset_.
