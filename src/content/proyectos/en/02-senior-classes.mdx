### Community Impact
- **Digital Inclusion**: I guided students from their first steps to the autonomous use of computing tools.
- **Cybersecurity Focus**: I prioritized teaching safe online practices to protect user integrity and privacy in the digital environment.
- **Tailored Methodology**: I designed specific content that addressed the particular needs and concerns of seniors, ensuring effective and meaningful learning.
